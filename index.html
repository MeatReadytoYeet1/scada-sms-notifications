SCADA Alarm Notification Service

Automated operational alarm and system notifications for authorized personnel.

Morgan Ellerbe provides automated SMS notifications related to VTScada and SCADA system alarms, equipment conditions, operational events, and system status.

SMS notifications are intended only for authorized personnel who have enrolled in the alarm notification service.

SMS Enrollment

Authorized personnel may voluntarily enroll by texting START to (479) 689-0322. By texting START, you consent to receive automated VTScada and SCADA operational alarm and system notifications from Morgan Ellerbe. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

SMS consent is not a condition of employment, purchase, or receipt of any other service.
