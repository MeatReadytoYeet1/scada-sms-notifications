SCADA Alarm Notification Service

Operated by Morgan Ellerbe

The SCADA Alarm Notification Service provides automated operational text-message notifications generated by VTScada and other SCADA systems. Notifications may include active alarms, cleared alarms, equipment conditions, communication failures, and system status information.

This service is intended only for authorized operators, employees, technicians, and other personnel who voluntarily choose to receive these notifications.

How to Opt In

To enroll, text:

START

to:

(479) 689-0322

Consent to Receive Messages

By texting START to (479) 689-0322, you agree to receive recurring automated SCADA alarm and operational notification text messages from Morgan Ellerbe.

Message frequency varies according to operational and alarm activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Stopping Messages

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from text notifications.

After opting out, you will receive a confirmation message and will receive no additional SCADA notification messages unless you subsequently opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance.

You may also contact the service administrator at:

(479) 689-0322

Supported Keywords

Opt-in keywords include: START, YES, and UNSTOP.

Opt-out keywords include: STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, REVOKE, OPTOUT, and END.

Help keywords include: HELP and INFO.

Important Notice

SMS notifications are intended to supplement normal SCADA alarming and operational procedures. SMS delivery may be delayed or unavailable because of cellular carrier outages, network conditions, equipment failures, or other circumstances outside the control of the service administrator.

SMS messaging should not be used as the sole notification mechanism for emergency, life-safety, or other critical functions.
