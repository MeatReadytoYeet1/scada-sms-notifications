Ellerbe LLC SCADA Alarm Notification Service Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Ellerbe LLC provides automated SMS notifications to authorized personnel regarding VTScada and SCADA alarms, equipment conditions, operational events, system status, and related notifications through the Ellerbe LLC SCADA Alarm Notification Service.

Program Description

Participants enrolled in the SCADA Alarm Notification Service may receive automated text messages related to operational alarms, equipment failures, system status, maintenance notifications, and other SCADA-related events.

Enrollment and Consent

Authorized personnel may voluntarily enroll by texting START to (479) 689-0322. The consent wording presented to users is: “To receive automated VTScada and SCADA alarm and system notifications from Ellerbe LLC, text START to (479) 689-0322. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.”

By texting START, you consent to receive automated operational alarm and system notifications from Ellerbe LLC at the mobile number used to enroll. SMS consent is not a condition of employment, purchase, or receipt of any other service.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on alarm activity, system conditions, operational events, and notification settings.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and wireless service plan.

Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP to an applicable message or by requesting removal from the SCADA alarm notification roster.

Help

Reply HELP to an applicable SMS message for assistance or contact Ellerbe LLC at (479) 689-0322.

Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery of SMS notifications may depend on cellular coverage, carrier availability, Internet connectivity, and other telecommunications services.

Operational Use

SMS notifications are provided as an operational notification mechanism. Recipients should follow established operating procedures for verifying, acknowledging, and responding to SCADA alarms and system conditions.

Privacy

Information associated with the SMS notification service is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Changes to These Terms

Ellerbe LLC may update these Terms & Conditions periodically to reflect changes to the notification service or applicable requirements.
