Purpose of the Service

The SCADA Alarm Notification Service provides automated SMS notifications relating to VTScada and other supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) systems.

Messages may include active alarms, cleared alarms, equipment conditions, communication failures, system status information, and other operational notifications.

Eligibility

The service is intended for authorized operators, employees, technicians, and other personnel who have been authorized to receive SCADA operational notifications.

SMS Enrollment and Consent

Users may enroll by texting START to (479) 689-0322.

By texting START, you agree to receive recurring automated SCADA alarm and operational notification text messages from Morgan Ellerbe.

Message frequency varies based on alarm and operational activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Opting Out

You may cancel SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP.

After sending STOP, you will receive a confirmation message and no additional messages will be sent unless you subsequently opt in again.

You may re-enroll by texting START to (479) 689-0322.
