Privacy Policy

SCADA Alarm Notification Service
Operated by Morgan Ellerbe

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morgan Ellerbe handles information associated with the SCADA Alarm Notification Service.

Information We Collect

When an authorized user participates in the SCADA Alarm Notification Service, information that may be collected or processed includes:

  • Mobile telephone number
  • SMS opt-in and opt-out status
  • Date and time of enrollment or withdrawal of consent
  • Messages or keywords sent to the service
  • Message delivery and status information

Operational SCADA information contained in notifications may include alarm descriptions, equipment status, site information, and other system-generated operational information.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to operate, administer, troubleshoot, and maintain the SCADA Alarm Notification Service.

Mobile telephone numbers are used to deliver requested SCADA alarm and operational notifications and to process opt-in, opt-out, and help requests.

SMS Consent and Mobile Information

Mobile information will not be sold or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS opt-in information and consent records will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be provided to service providers when necessary to operate the messaging service, including telecommunications and messaging providers that transmit and deliver SMS messages. Such information is provided only as necessary to perform those services.

Twilio

The SCADA Alarm Notification Service uses Twilio as a communications service provider for SMS transmission and delivery. Information necessary to send and receive SMS messages may therefore be processed by Twilio and applicable telecommunications carriers.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain consent records, troubleshoot messaging issues, meet operational requirements, or satisfy applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information associated with the service. However, no electronic communications or information system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

Participation in the SMS notification service is voluntary.

Users may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to a message from the service.

Users may subsequently re-enroll by texting START to (479) 689-0322.
