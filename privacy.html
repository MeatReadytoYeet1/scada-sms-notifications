SCADA Alarm Notification Service Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Morgan Ellerbe provides automated SMS notifications to authorized personnel regarding VTScada and SCADA operational alarms, equipment conditions, system status, and other related events through the SCADA Alarm Notification Service operated by Morgan Ellerbe.

Information We Collect

Morgan Ellerbe may collect the mobile telephone number, name, notification preferences, and SMS opt-in and consent records of personnel who enroll in the SCADA Alarm Notification Service.

How Information Is Used

Mobile telephone numbers and related information are used solely to provide requested operational notifications, VTScada and SCADA alarm messages, system status notifications, and related administrative communications.

SMS Consent and Mobile Information

Morgan Ellerbe does not sell, rent, share, or provide mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be provided to service providers when necessary to operate the notification service, including telecommunications and messaging providers used to deliver requested SMS messages.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information associated with the notification service from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Opting Out

Participants may opt out of SMS notifications by replying STOP to an applicable SMS message or by requesting removal from the SCADA alarm notification roster.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the SMS notification service, contact Morgan Ellerbe at (479) 689-0322.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Ellerbe LLC may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to the notification service or applicable requirements.
