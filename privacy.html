Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

The SCADA Alarm Notification Service provides automated SMS notifications to authorized personnel regarding operational alarms, equipment conditions, system status, and other SCADA-related events.

Information We Collect

We may collect the mobile telephone number, name, and notification preferences of personnel who enroll in the SCADA Alarm Notification Service.

How Information Is Used

Mobile telephone numbers and related information are used solely for providing operational notifications, SCADA alarm messages, system status notifications, and related administrative communications.

SMS Consent and Mobile Information

Mobile information, including telephone numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent, will not be sold, rented, shared, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be provided to service providers when necessary to operate the notification service, including telecommunications and messaging providers used to deliver requested SMS messages.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information associated with the notification service from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Opting Out

Participants may opt out of SMS notifications by replying STOP to an applicable SMS message or by requesting removal from the SCADA alarm notification roster.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the notification service or applicable requirements.
